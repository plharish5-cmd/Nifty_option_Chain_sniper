RVOL SCANNER
=============================================================================
Which stocks are unusually busy today — measured against their own normal.

WHAT IT DOES
  For every stock it compares TODAY's volume so far against what that SAME
  stock normally does BY THE SAME TIME OF DAY, averaged over its last 10
  completed sessions. That ratio is RVOL.

WHY TIME OF DAY MATTERS
  Volume is U-shaped through the session: heavy at the open, quiet through
  lunch, heavy into the close. Measured against a whole-day average, every
  stock looks asleep at 10am and frantic at 3pm — you would be measuring the
  clock, not the stock.

WHY THE FLOOR IS IN RUPEES, NOT SHARES
  MARUTI traded 29,442 shares by 09:45 on 25 Aug 2026. That is under any sane
  share-count floor — yet it is Rs 39.8 CRORE of real turnover. Meanwhile a
  Rs-50 stock doing 1 lakh shares is Rs 50 lakh and genuinely thin. Filtering
  on share count silently deletes every expensive stock, so this filters on
  rupees.

WHAT IT IS NOT
  It does not tell you direction. It never says buy or sell. High RVOL means
  SOMETHING is happening — results, news, a block deal, an institution working
  an order. It is a stock-SELECTION tool. What you do next is your business.

QUICK START
  1. Double-click RVOLScanner.exe — the browser opens by itself.
  2. Paste your Dhan access token in Setup.
  3. Press Scan. Click any stock to see today's volume curve against its usual.

  Your token is stored in config.json next to this program and is sent only to
  Dhan's own API. Nothing goes anywhere else.
