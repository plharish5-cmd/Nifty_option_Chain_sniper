## 1. Executive Philosophy & Purpose of View Building

> *"When you start building your view, you will have that clarity: OK, when do I have to invest? When do I enter? When the view building gives you clarity, that is the time you trade. Live market is completely different from historical charts — sitting outside the swimming pool versus diving into the water."*

View Building is the institutional pre-market diagnostic routine designed to transform market noise into a structured, mathematical bias before the opening bell rings. 
- **Time Horizon:** Formulated pre-market (between 21:00 the previous night and 07:30–08:00 AM IST on the trading day).
- **Core Principle:** Individual retail traders make up $<0.0000000000001\%$ of the market. The market is moved by FIIs, DIIs, and institutional derivative desks rotating billions. View Building measures institutional demand, raw material costs, volatility regimes, option seller commitments, and global cues to establish the daily playbook.
- **Outcome:** A total weighted score (Bullish, Bearish, or Neutral) and an anticipated **Market Shape** (V-Shape, A-Shape, W-Shape, M-Shape, or Sideways Chop).

## 3. Granular Analysis of Core Parameters & Mentor Insights

### Parameter 1 & 2: Spot vs Futures Basis (Demand & Derivative Mechanics)
- **The Derivative Advantage:** Cash market delivery of 650 shares of a heavyweight (e.g. HDFC Bank) requires ~₹4,62,000 capital. In derivatives, the same exposure is controlled with ~₹82,000 margin.
- **Where Big Money Rotates:** Institutional players (FIIs, DIIs, Mutual Funds) rotate capital in the derivative market rather than keeping it stagnant in cash. Retailers account for a tiny fraction ($<0.0000000000001\%$).
- **Premium vs Discount Rule:**
  - **Premium ($\text{Futures} > \text{Spot}$):** Institutional buyers are willing to pay extra for future contracts. **Signals institutional demand &bull; Bullish.**
  - **Discount ($\text{Futures} < \text{Spot}$):** Futures trading below spot indicates aggressive selling/hedging with lack of buyers. **Signals lack of demand &bull; Avoid buying long.**
- **Bank Nifty's Unique Importance:** Banks provide the primary input for all businesses — **Capital** (Capital first, Raw Materials second, Labor third). If Bank Nifty futures show premium, capital flows are healthy, propelling Nifty 50 upward.
- **The Divergence Signal:** If the Spot index closed slightly negative (e.g. -10 pts) but the Futures closed positive (+60 pts), derivative demand is actively absorbing spot weakness!

### Parameter 3: India VIX & Fear Dynamics
- **What VIX Measures:** Market volatility and investor fear.
- **The Shift Threshold ($\pm 4.0\%$):**
  - **Change $< -4.0\%$ (e.g., -5.82%):** Fear is evaporating. Less volatility gives confidence to institutional buyers to enter and hold longs &rarr; **Score: +2 (Bullish)**.
  - **Change $> +4.0\%$ (e.g., +6.5%):** Panic/uncertainty expanding. Buyers flee or exit longs; price action setups face high failure risk &rarr; **Score: -2 (Bearish)**.
  - **Change between $-4.0\%$ and $+4.0\%$:** Baseline volatility regime &rarr; **Score: 0 (Neutral)**.

### Parameter 4: Macroeconomics of Crude Oil (The ₹6,500 Benchmark)
- **India's Energy Dependency:** India produces only 4%–6% of its oil consumption domestically and imports 94%–96%.
- **Corporate Economics:**
  - **Crude $< 6,500$ ₹/barrel ($< \$65/bbl$):** Low raw material and transportation costs. Corporate margins expand, earnings rise, companies hire and invest &rarr; **Score: +1 (Bullish)**.
  - **Crude $> 6,500$ ₹/barrel ($> \$65/bbl$):** Energy costs erode profit margins. Companies spend money paying for oil rather than business growth, triggering cost cuts and layoffs &rarr; **Score: -1 (Bearish)**.

### Parameter 5: GIFT Nifty Gap Prediction — The Golden Rule
- **The Common Retail Mistake:** Retail traders incorrectly compare GIFT Nifty with Nifty 50 Spot. **Mentor screams: THAT IS COMPLETELY WRONG.**
- **The Correct Institutional Benchmark:**
  $$\Delta = \text{GIFT Nifty at 07:30 AM IST} - \text{NIFTY CURRENT MONTH FUTURES CLOSE}$$
- **The 3 Opening States:**
  1. **GAP UP:** $\Delta \ge +100\text{ points}$. International demand is high &rarr; Expect gap up across Nifty, Bank Nifty, and constituent stocks.
  2. **GAP DOWN:** $\Delta \le -100\text{ points}$. International demand is weak &rarr; Expect gap down.
  3. **FLAT OPEN:** $-100 < \Delta < +100\text{ points}$ (e.g., $-49\text{ pts}$, $+35\text{ pts}$, $-60\text{ pts}$).

### Parameter 13: Put-Call Ratio (PCR) & Option Seller Mechanics
- **Formula:**
  $$\text{PCR} = \frac{\text{Total Put Open Interest}}{\text{Total Call Open Interest}}$$
- **Reading Seller Psychology:** Option chain totals represent **SELLERS (WRITERS)**, not retail buyers:
  - Put Sellers = Bullish commitment (betting market won't fall below strike).
  - Call Sellers = Bearish commitment (betting market won't rise above strike).
- **The Extreme Reversal Zones:**
  - **$\text{PCR} < 0.5$ (Oversold):** Call sellers vastly outnumber put sellers (e.g. 20L vs 10L). They push price down initially. When price hits key support (S1/S2), sellers book profit (buy to cover), put sellers step in, and short-covering triggers a violent **V-SHAPED (or W-SHAPED) RECOVERY** back up to resistance (100–200+ pts).
  - **$\text{PCR} > 1.4$ (Overbought):** Put sellers dominate early and push price up. At major resistance (R1/R2), they lock profits and flip to call writing. The market dumps into an **A-SHAPED (or M-SHAPED) SELL-OFF** down to support.
  - **$0.5 \le \text{PCR} \le 1.4$:** Neutral balanced market.

### Parameter 14: Institutional FII Flow Confluence & Volatile Day Dynamics
- **3 Components:** FII/DII Cash (Cr), FII Index Futures (Cr / Contracts), FII Stock Futures (Cr / Contracts).
- **Net Derivative Flow:** Index Futures + Stock Futures.
- **The Two-Sided Volatile Market Rule:**
  - When FIIs **sell Index Futures** (bearish hedge/dump) but simultaneously **buy Cash Equities or Stock Futures** (accumulating individual companies):
  - Do NOT expect a continuous one-way crash.
  - The market will frequently gap down, find aggressive support, stage a sharp morning bounce, and trade in a **two-sided volatile structure (W-pattern)**.

## 4. Master Market Shape Playbook (V, A, W, M Patterns)

```
       V-SHAPE RECOVERY (Oversold)              A-SHAPE COLLAPSE (Overbought)
       ---------------------------              -----------------------------
  Open                                                     Peak Resistance (R1/R2)
    \                                                              /\
     \                                                            /  \
      \                                                          /    \
       \__ Support (S1/S2) -> Buy                               /      \__ Support (S1)
          \___________/                                        /
                      \                                  Open /
                       \______> Target Resistance (R1)
  Trigger: Stoch RSI < 20 AND/OR PCR < 0.5    Trigger: Stoch RSI > 80 AND/OR PCR > 1.4

       W-SHAPE PATTERN (Two-Sided)              M-SHAPE PATTERN (Two-Sided)
       ---------------------------              ---------------------------
  Open       Bounce to 8 EMA                   Peak 1     Pullback to 8 EMA    Peak 2 (Double Top)
    \           /\                               /\           /\                 \
     \         /  \                             /  \         /  \                 \
      \_______/    \_______> Rallies           /    \_______/    \                 \_______> Support
      Bottom 1     Bottom 2 (Double Bottom)   Open
  Trigger: FII Cash Buying + Fut Selling       Trigger: FII Cash Selling + Exhaustion at R2
```

1. **V-Shape Market (Oversold Bounce):**
   - **Drivers:** Daily Stoch RSI $< 20$ and/or Option Chain $\text{PCR} < 0.5$.
   - **Trajectory:** Initial morning selling drives price into major Support (S1 or S2). Price consolidates in demand zone, prints a Hammer / Doji, and explodes upward toward Resistance (R1/R2) for +100 to +200 points.
2. **A-Shape Market (Overbought Rejection):**
   - **Drivers:** Daily Stoch RSI $> 80$ and/or Option Chain $\text{PCR} > 1.4$.
   - **Trajectory:** Strong morning open or early push up exhausts at major Resistance (R1 or R2). Price prints Inverted Hammer / Shooting Star, rolls over, and dumps +100 to +200 points down to Support.
3. **W-Shape Market (Double Bottom Reversal):**
   - Initial selloff finds support &rarr; Bounces to test 8 EMA &rarr; Pulls back to retest initial low (Double Bottom) &rarr; Confirms reversal & rallies into close.
4. **M-Shape Market (Double Top Rejection):**
   - Initial rally hits resistance &rarr; Pulls back to test 8 EMA &rarr; Second push fails at resistance (Double Top) &rarr; Collapses into close.
