## 📌 ZONE TYPES & ACTIONS

### **1. CONFLUENCE ZONE (★ A+)**
**What it is:** Monthly + Weekly + Daily support/resistance align at same price

**Hover shows:**
```
🎯 PRIME ENTRY ZONE
Entry: Close above this + stacked nodes confirmation
Target: Next confluence zone (±2ATR minimum)
SL: Just below the zone
Risk/Reward: Minimum 1:2
```

**What you do:**
- ✓ **WAIT** for stacked nodes ENTRY badge at this price
- ✓ **WATCH** for daily close above VWAP +2σ + zone
- ✓ **BUY** when both confirm (highest probability trade)
- ✓ Use **fixed SL** at zone low, not trailing
- ✓ Take **50% profit** at VWAP, ride 50% to next zone

**Probability:** 70%+ win rate when confluence + nodes + order flow all agree

### **2. MONTHLY ZONE**
**Hover shows:**
```
📊 MACRO SUPPORT/RESISTANCE
Action: Confluence validation point
Entry: Only if weekly also has zone here
SL: 1.5 ATR below zone
Risk/Reward: Minimum 1:3 on breakout
```

**When price arrives here:**
- ⚠️ **PAUSE** — Check if weekly also has zone
  - YES → Strong hold likely, **BUY dips**
  - NO → Price may pierce through → **Wait for pullback**
- 🔍 **LOOK FOR** volume spikes (trapped traders)

**Speed detection:**
- **Thin volume between zones?** Price ACCELERATES here
- **Thick volume cluster at zone?** Price REVERSES here

### **3. WEEKLY ZONE**
**Hover shows:**
```
⚡ INTERMEDIATE EQUILIBRIUM
Action: Primary bounce point (70% success rate)
Entry: Daily ±2σ dip into this zone
SL: Just below zone
Risk/Reward: Minimum 1:1.5
```

**When price arrives here:**
- 📈 **HIGH PROBABILITY BOUNCE** (if daily hasn't bounced yet)
- 🔴 **FALSE BREAKOUT RISK** (if volume drops on the break)
  - Check order flow: Are there big trades?
  - Check daily: Did close occur outside zone?
  - **If both NO** → Rejection likely → **SELL into strength**
- 🟢 **CONFIRMED SUPPORT** (if price bounces with volume)
  - **Take 50% at ±1σ**
  - **Ride 50% to next resistance**

**Common patterns at weekly zones:**
- **Inside bar + rejection wick** = False breakout incoming
- **Stacked imbalance + volume cluster** = True reversal
- **Big trades at zone with low bar volume** = Institutional support/resistance

### **4. DAILY ZONE**
**Hover shows:**
```
🎯 INTRADAY ACTION ZONE
Action: Immediate entry/exit point
Entry: ±2σ stretch into zone + close confirmation
SL: Daily low or VWAP -2σ
Risk/Reward: 1:1 minimum, 1:1.5 ideal
```

**Real-time decision:**

| Price Action | Action | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **Price in zone + volume surges** | 🟢 **BUY** | Trapped traders pushing through |
| **Price breaks zone + volume DROPS** | 🔴 **SELL** | False breakout = reversal coming |
| **Price bounces off zone 3x** | ⛔ **SKIP** | Might break next attempt → wait for confirmation |
| **Big trade absorption at zone** | 🟢 **BUY** | Institutional buying = support valid |
| **Rejection wick above zone** | 🔴 **SELL** | Cannot hold above = will return |

**Time-based guidance:**
- **Morning (9:15–11:00):** Follow daily zones strictly (lowest noise)
- **Mid-day (11:00–14:00):** Tight SLs only (higher volatility)
- **Afternoon (14:00–15:30):** Volatility contracts, reversals sharper (best for entries)
