## 3. The app in one paragraph

Two desks. **Port 8510** = the algo execution terminal (`engine_8510_live.py` FastAPI backend + `analyst/terminal8510_backend.py` + the single-file cockpit `Algorithmic_Trading_Workspace_8510.html`). **Port 8600** = the ICT/SMC option-chain desk (the locked modules) which 8510 reads from. **Port 8501** = an older Streamlit option-chain view. 8510 records its own spot candles and option-chain snapshots to SQLite for backtest/replay.

### The 6 strategies ("Equity Guys" = the first 5)
1. **Stage 2 8-EMA (Long)** — UBB up 2 bars, 4>8, first touch of the dynamic 8 EMA.
2. **Stage 3 8-EMA (Short)** — LBB down 2 bars, 4<8, first touch.
3. **8 EMA Reversal Up/Down** — 4/8 cross, wait 2 bars, touch within 2 bars.
4. **1 HR Flagship (PA)** — daily S/R + 1H swing + closed 15m PA.
5. **8 EMA Prototype** — the owner's main focus (see §4).
6. **8600 Ticket lane** — an 8600 Dip Buy / Pullback Sell ticket opens a trade (off by default).

Exit for Stage/Reversal/Prototype/Ticket: **±40 spot OR ±40 option points, whichever first, + opposite 15m close + 15:20 square-off.** (Reversal uses ±20.) Flagship exits at S/R levels.

## 4. The 8 EMA Prototype — exact rules (owner-set, do not change without a prompt)

State machine: **15m close crosses the 8 EMA → ARM (no entry on the cross candle) → price moves ≥2 pts away → entry on the touch (±3 pts) of the 8 EMA**, where the 8 EMA is recomputed on live NIFTY spot (now every 3 seconds). Then:
- **Profit (+40):** re-enter the same side on the next touch.
- **Stop-loss (−40) or opposite 15m close:** that side is cancelled; the opposite side arms on the next opposite close.
- **One trade at a time.** Entries 09:30–15:20. Square off 15:20.
- Contract: current-week CE/PE with **premium ≥ ₹200**; if the 4 matrix strikes are all under ₹200 it walks deeper ITM in 50-pt steps (v2.0.18).
- **No hard cap on trades/day.** 13 Nov chart = 5 trades, +40/−40/+40/+40/+35 ≈ **+115 pts**, all reachable on defaults.
- Cockpit toggles (all persist to `scanner.ema8_prototype` in config): Ribbon (block if 4–8 within 3 pts), EMA separation (long only 4>8 / short only 4<8), SuperTrend, Retouch-requires-away, Slow-market exact line, **Re-entry after profit ON/OFF**, **Re-entry after stop-loss: side-cancelled-flip-only / same-side-again**. The OI filter placeholder reads `oi_bias` from the 8600 Board.
